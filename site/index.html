About us

Learn More About Our Services

ADD Home Healthcare is dedicated to providing supportive services tailored to individual needs. We understand that every person and every home is different. Our goal is to help individuals manage everyday activities, build independence, and experience a better quality of life with dignity and respect.

Through personalized support and practical assistance, we help make daily living more comfortable and manageable.

Our services

Personalized Support for Everyday Living

We offer a range of support services designed to assist individuals with daily activities, household needs, independence, and transitions in their living arrangements.

  1. 01

    Personal Care

    Support with everyday personal activities to help individuals maintain comfort, dignity, and independence in their daily routines.

  2. 02

    Homemaker Services

    Practical household assistance to help maintain a clean, organized, and comfortable living environment.

  3. 03

    In-Home Support Services (IHSS)

    Individualized support to help people manage daily living activities and remain as independent as possible in their homes.

  4. 04

    Transition Coordination Services

    Guidance and coordination to help individuals navigate changes in living arrangements, services, and support needs.

  5. 05

    Household Setup

    Assistance with organizing household essentials and preparing living spaces to support a comfortable and functional home.

  6. 06

    Life Skills Trainers (LST)

    Practical guidance to help individuals develop everyday living skills, build confidence, and strengthen their independence.
